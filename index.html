01About

Working towards clinical tools that clinicians can understand and check

My PhD develops EEG-based screening and triage tools and systems that support clinical decisions in neurology, with a focus on dementia and epilepsy. I aim for tools that are interpretable, so a clinician can see which features of the signal led to a result, and that keep working on patients from hospitals not used to build them.

Beyond EEG, I build automated processing pipelines for different medical imaging modalities, and lightweight AI agents that run locally on a laptop GPU. At Hridae Medical Technologies, which I co-founded, we develop wearable monitoring and ECG models for precision cardiology and neurology. I studied physiology and neuroscience before engineering. I collaborate with clinicians and researchers at the Institute of Medical Sciences (BHU), AIIMS New Delhi, IIT Kanpur and IIT Kharagpur.

Speaker bio +

Soinik Ghosh is a PhD researcher at the School of Biomedical Engineering, IIT (BHU) Varanasi, and a co-founder of Hridae Medical Technologies. The PhD focuses on EEG-based screening, triage and clinical decision-support tools for dementia and epilepsy. Work on reduced-electrode EEG for cognitive screening, carried out with neurologists at the Institute of Medical Sciences, BHU, was accepted at the GlobalSouthAI workshop at NeurIPS 2026. Soinik also builds automated processing pipelines for medical images and lightweight AI agents that run locally. Hridae develops wearable monitoring and ECG models for precision cardiology and neurology. Collaborators include clinicians and researchers at IMS BHU, AIIMS New Delhi, IIT Kanpur and IIT Kharagpur. Soinik studied physiology and neuroscience at the University of Calcutta before moving into engineering.

02Featured

GlobalSouthAI @ NeurIPS 2026 · Accepted

Decomposing a clinical EEG montage for low-channel cognitive triage

In recordings re-derived to simulate low-channel devices, a few well-placed electrodes screened for cognitive impairment about as well as a full clinical EEG. This points towards simpler screening devices, which still need testing as real hardware.

S. Ghosh, R. N. Chaurasia, S. Sharma, N. Sharma

03Research

Current work

Research

  1. EEG

    Screening and triage for dementia

    Tools that help clinicians decide who needs a full cognitive assessment, using simpler EEG recordings.

  2. EEG

    Epilepsy monitoring and seizure forecasting

    Methods that adapt to each patient's EEG to detect seizures and estimate when seizure risk is rising.

  3. Imaging

    Automated medical image processing

    Pipelines that prepare, check and analyse images from different modalities with few manual steps, so imaging studies run the same way each time.

Building

  1. AI agents

    Lightweight local AI agents

    Agents that run entirely on an 8 GB laptop GPU, so data stay on the device. They plan and complete multi-step coding, research and document tasks.

  2. Wearables

    Precision cardiology and neurology at Hridae

    Wearable monitoring devices, ECG models, patient-specific heart models and analysis software, developed with clinicians.

    hridae.com
  3. Cognition

    Cognitive assessment app

    Short cognitive tasks that run offline on a phone or laptop and track changes in each person over time. Designed to support screening, not to diagnose.
