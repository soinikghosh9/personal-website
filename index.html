01About

Turning brain signals into clinical decisions that clinicians can understand and trust

My PhD develops EEG-based screening and triage tools and intelligent systems that support clinical decisions in neurology, with a focus on dementia and epilepsy. I aim for tools that are interpretable, so a clinician can see which features of the signal led to a result, and reliable, so they keep working on patients from hospitals that were not used to build them.

Beyond EEG, I build automated processing pipelines for different medical imaging modalities, and a personal AI agent that runs locally on a laptop GPU, which I am training to carry out coding, research and clinical workflow tasks. At Hridae Medical Technologies, which I co-founded, we develop wearable monitoring for precision cardiology and neurology. I studied physiology and neuroscience before engineering, and I work closely with neurologists at the Institute of Medical Sciences, BHU.

Speaker bio +

Soinik Ghosh is a PhD researcher at the School of Biomedical Engineering, IIT (BHU) Varanasi, and Co-Founder and CTO of Hridae Medical Technologies. The PhD focuses on EEG-based screening and triage tools and clinical decision-support systems that are interpretable and reliable. Soinik also builds automated processing pipelines for medical images and a local AI agent, running on a laptop GPU, for research and clinical workflows. Hridae develops wearable monitoring for precision cardiology and neurology. Work on reduced-electrode EEG for cognitive screening, carried out with neurologists at the Institute of Medical Sciences, BHU, was accepted at the GlobalSouthAI workshop at NeurIPS 2026. Soinik studied physiology and neuroscience at the University of Calcutta before moving into engineering.

03Research

Current work

Research

  1. EEG

    Screening and triage for dementia

    Tools that help clinicians decide who needs a full cognitive assessment, using simpler EEG recordings.

  2. EEG

    Epilepsy monitoring and seizure forecasting

    Methods that adapt to each patient's EEG to detect seizures and estimate when seizure risk is rising.

  3. Imaging

    Automated medical image processing

    Pipelines that prepare, check and analyse images from different medical imaging modalities with minimal manual steps, so imaging studies can be run consistently and at scale.

Building

  1. AI agents

    A local AI agent for research and clinical work

    A personal coding and research agent that runs entirely on an 8 GB laptop GPU, so data stay on the device. I am training its workflow to plan and complete multi-step tasks, from literature research and analysis code to complex clinical workflows.

  2. Wearables

    Precision cardiology and neurology at Hridae

    Wearable monitoring devices and analysis software, developed with clinicians.

    hridae.com
  3. Cognition

    Cognitive assessment app

    Short cognitive tasks that run offline on a phone or laptop and track changes in each person over time. Designed to support screening, not to diagnose.
