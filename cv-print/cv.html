Research Profile

PhD researcher in biomedical engineering working on AI for clinical neurophysiology and medical imaging. My doctoral research develops EEG-based screening, triage and decision-support tools for dementia and epilepsy, with an emphasis on interpretable signal features and testing on independent clinical cohorts. This includes first-author work on reduced-electrode EEG for cognitive screening, accepted at GlobalSouthAI @ NeurIPS 2026, and on patient-adaptive seizure detection, published in Computers in Biology and Medicine. I also build automated medical imaging pipelines, and I am building a lightweight local agentic harness and orchestration layer on open models, for long and complex tasks. As a co-founder of Hridae Medical Technologies, I work on wearable monitoring, ECG models and ML-accelerated digital twins from medical images for precision cardiology and neurology. I trained in physiology and neuroscience before moving into engineering.

Featured Research

Low-channel EEG for cognitive triageGlobalSouthAI @ NeurIPS 2026 • IIT (BHU), IMS BHU

Studied how far a clinical EEG can be simplified for cognitive screening, using memory-clinic recordings re-derived to simulate low-channel devices and independent cohorts from other hospitals. A few well-placed electrodes screened about as well as the full clinical montage, and EEG added value beyond age alone; detecting mild cognitive impairment remains an open problem. Proposes a simplified recording design for prospective device studies.
